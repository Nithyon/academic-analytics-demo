    Columns marked 🔒 hold personal data. They exist in the database but are not on the allow-list, so the validator rejects any query that uses them.

    Upload faculty Excel sheets attendance and marks, one workbook per course and section

    Download a sample workbook

    Each workbook has three sheets. Info: course_code, section_code, term. Attendance: Roll No, Name, then one column per class hour such as 2026-08-12 P3 with P, A, OD or ML. Marks: Roll No, Name, then one column per paper item such as Lab test | Lab test viva (5) with the mark, AB (absent) or W (withheld). Uploading a workbook again replaces that course and section for that term. Converted marks, totals, grades and attendance percentages are recomputed with the rules in backend/rules.py.
